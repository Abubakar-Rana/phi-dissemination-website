The Planetary Health Informatics Lab

We study how a changing environment changes human health, and we build the data, models and tools that let others act on what we find.

What we do

Planetary health, read from data

Planetary health research looks at how human health is linked to changes in the natural environment. We combine artificial intelligence, satellite imagery and real world health records to understand how climate and environmental change affect disease, both infectious and chronic, and to find the causes of health inequalities between populations and countries.

Our work spans malaria forecasting, floods and disasters, heat and child health, genomics and clinical AI. Each study is published here as a visual story with its data, methods and findings set out in full.

The lab sits within the Nuffield Department of Orthopaedics, Rheumatology and Musculoskeletal Sciences at the University of Oxford, and works with partners across South Asia and Africa.
