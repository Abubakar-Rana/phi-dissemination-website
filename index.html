AI for Planet and Health

We use AI, satellite imagery and real-world data to understand how climate and environmental change affect health, including chronic and infectious disease, and to find the causes of health inequalities across populations and countries.

The lab

How a changing planet changes our health

Planetary health research looks at how human health is linked to changes in the natural environment. We build the datasets, models and open tools that turn satellite and health data into evidence decision-makers can act on.

The lab is led by Sara Khalid, Associate Professor of Health Informatics and Biomedical Data Science, at NDORMS, University of Oxford, and works with partners across South Asia and Africa.
